20TUE

People’s Square → the Bund

Museum, a snack walk, then the river

Shanghai Museum → Nanjing Road → the Bund

Start close to home with the museum if its current exhibition appeals. Walk east along Nanjing Road, stopping for snacks rather than a shopping circuit. Finish by the river as daylight fades.

Eat: try shengjian at Da Hu Chun; the Sichuan Middle Road shop fits near the south end of the Bund. Park Hotel butterfly pastries fit the People’s Square end.

About 3–5 km spread across the day. Take Line 2 back from East Nanjing Road to People’s Square, or a taxi.

Museum ticket & an important choice

This is the building on your map: People’s Square, 201 Renmin Avenue. During your trip it hosts On Top of the World Tree: Ancient Civilizations of the Americas. The current notice says the whole building is devoted to this exhibition and all visitors need a ticket.

How to book: use 上海博物馆 official WeChat → GO上博 → 参观预约 → People’s Square / exhibition tickets. Select the date, ticket and passport details requested. Keep the confirmation and bring both original passports. Follow the ticket’s current entry conditions.

If you wanted the museum’s Chinese antiquities instead, use the East building option on 25 October and keep this morning as a park-and-breakfast morning. Don’t buy a ticket for the wrong collection.

Suzhou: a garden & canal lanes

Jiangsu day trip · sleep in Shanghai

Humble Administrator’s Garden → Pingjiang Road

Make one classical garden the main visit, then walk south into the canal lanes for lunch, small shops and a tea break. Pingjiang Road is enough for this afternoon; don’t add a second canal district just to tick it off.

Eat: Yu Xing Ji, Xibei Street / 西北街店, for noodles near the garden, then a small sweet snack or tea along Pingjiang Road.

About 3–5 km in Suzhou. Return when we have had enough, with a comfortable buffer for the booked train; dinner can be back in Shanghai.

Train & garden bookings

Train: compare Shanghai Railway Station 上海站 → Suzhou 苏州站 first. Shanghai station is near this hotel by Line 1; Hongqiao is an alternative if its train is more suitable. Check the full station names, not just the city.

As a planning allowance, expect about 30–60 minutes on a suitable high-speed train, plus transfers and security. Use 12306’s actual timetable when released. Arrive at the departure station roughly 45–60 minutes before the train, with original passports.

Booking date: start checking 7 October for travel on 21 October under the current 15-day inclusive release window. Book both directions.

Garden: WeChat → verified 苏州园林旅游 official platform → attraction tickets → 拙政园 → date/time → passport details → payment. Advance online reservation is required. Check the live calendar for its release window; if there is no passport option, ask the hotel or the attraction before purchasing a different identity type.

Optional: Suzhou Museum, if we prefer it

The main museum 苏州博物馆本馆 is beside the garden. Treat it as a replacement for the long garden visit, or keep both visits short and skip extra stops.

How to book: use the official website or WeChat mini program 苏州博物馆全预约, choose 本馆, and enter the passport details for each person. Admission is free but a timed reservation is required. If sold out, keep the garden-and-Pingjiang plan. The West museum is a different, distant branch.

Leafy streets & Ren He Guan

French Concession area · our anchor meal

Wukang Mansion → Wukang Road → Anfu Road → taxi → Ren He Guan, Xuhui

Start at Wukang Mansion and follow Wukang Road north towards Anfu Road. Stop whenever a café or side street looks inviting. Take a taxi to dinner rather than turning this into a long cross-town walk.

Must-eat: 人和馆（徐汇店），肇嘉浜路407号. Share crab-roe rice, one meat dish and a vegetable; confirm portion sizes before adding more.

About 2–4 km of optional walking, plus a short taxi leg. Start with Line 1 to Hengshan Road and walk, or take a taxi directly to Wukang Mansion.

How to book Ren He Guan

Ask the hotel to call 021-64030731 for the Xuhui branch and request a table for two on 22 October. Confirm whether they accept reservations or issue same-day queue numbers, when queueing opens, and whether crab-roe rice must be ordered ahead.

23FRI

Yu Garden & the old city

All three old-city pins, in one compact area

Yu Garden → Lvbo Lang saved pin → City God Temple

Visit the classical garden, browse the lanes around the temple and leave room for a sit-down lunch if we want one. Keep the garden and temple as the two main stops.

Eat: Lvbo Lang is an optional lunch, with an outdated branch name flagged below. For dumplings alone, the recent reports read favour Lai Lai near our hotel; we can eat there before or after the old-city outing.

About 2–3 km locally. Take Line 8 one stop to Dashijie, then Line 14 one stop to Yuyuan Garden; a taxi is simpler if we prefer fewer station walks.

Garden, temple & restaurant: separate places

Yu Garden: buy through the official 上海豫园 WeChat account (ID yu-garden) or the South Gate ticket office at 279 Yuyuan Old Street. Choose the daytime garden ticket and the correct date; follow the passport requirements and bring originals. Monday is the normal closure day, so Friday fits.

City God Temple: the functioning temple has a separate entrance/ticket arrangement; buy through its entrance ticket office if we want to go inside. Walking the surrounding bazaar is a different visit and does not mean we have entered the temple or garden.

Lvbo Lang: our map’s “new building” name appears outdated; the branch note explains why. If choosing its current main restaurant at 豫园路115号, ask the hotel to call 021-63280602 for two people on 23 October. Confirm the à-la-carte dumpling/点心 menu, any minimum spend, and whether they accept a reservation or use a walk-in queue. A package or voucher does not guarantee a seat.

24SAT

Hongkou & the North Bund

A slower river day

North Bund riverside → Xiahai Temple / Hongkou lanes

Take a taxi to the North Bund and enjoy the open waterfront. Wander a few neighbouring streets for lunch and a café stop, then return to the riverside if the light is good. The landmark here is the view, so there is no need to add another paid attraction.

Eat: Yun Lai Ji’s vegetarian noodles by Xiahai Temple are a simple local option. Confirm the kitchen’s service hours first; if lunch ends early, eat before the river walk. Choose a café or sweet snack along the way rather than adding a cross-town detour.

Choose a 2–4 km loop. A taxi from the hotel keeps this outing easy. Use Amap for the return route from wherever we finish.

Tickets & a shorter version

No attraction booking is needed for the public riverside walk. If we are tired, just do the waterfront and one nearby meal. North Bund cafés and restaurants may need their own reservation; a terrace seat should not be assumed.

The North Bund is on the north/east side of the Suzhou Creek mouth. Keep it on this day instead of adding the detour to the main Bund walk.

25SUN

Pudong: up high, then by the water

Oriental Pearl & Lujiazui riverside

Oriental Pearl Tower → Lujiazui riverside

Take Line 2 directly to Lujiazui. Go up the Oriental Pearl if visibility is good, then come back down for a relaxed riverside walk looking towards the Bund. This is our one planned paid skyline viewpoint.

Eat: start with Lai Lai’s crab-and-pork dumplings near the hotel, then take Line 2 to Pudong. After the river, return towards the hotel for dinner or repeat a favourite. Leave enough room between a meal queue and any booked tower entry.

About 2–4 km. Sunday can be busy: avoid making sunset entry a fixed promise, and check queue and ticket conditions.

How to buy the right tower ticket

Open the verified 东方明珠 official WeChat service account → its current ticket menu. Select the sightseeing package with the viewing levels we actually want, date and passport details. Check whether the glass-floor experience is included; packages differ. Keep the confirmation.

The public official information I could verify does not establish the current foreign-passport checkout flow or a fixed ticket release time. If the form fails, ask the hotel to call the tower’s visitor hotline 021-58792888 for the accepted purchase/entry method. Do this before relying on a same-day ticket.

For haze or rain, move this outing to the spare day or another clear day, checking the ticket’s change/refund terms first.

Optional museum swap: Chinese antiquities

If we skip the People’s Square exhibition, Shanghai Museum East is the place for the Chinese antiquities collection. Give it its own unhurried visit, then choose either the tower or the riverside rather than squeezing all three in.

Take Line 2 from People’s Square to Shanghai Science and Technology Museum station and follow Amap to the East building, then use a taxi or metro to Lujiazui if continuing. They are in the same broad district, not next door.

General individual entry is currently walk-in with original ID, 10am–6pm, last entry 5pm. Closed Tuesdays; 25 October is Sunday. Selected interactive experiences and paid exhibitions may require separate booking through 上海博物馆 → GO上博 → 参观预约.

Optional Hangzhou: a little West Lake

Zhejiang day trip · only if we feel like another outing

Broken Bridge → Bai Causeway → Gushan

A second province, open water and a change of pace. Take a lakeside stroll along the north shore and Bai Causeway, then sit down for tea. Turn back or take a taxi whenever we have had enough.

Eat: Zhong Er noodles on Baochu Road is a convenient, mixed-review option before Broken Bridge, followed by a tea break near the lake. Keep food stops on this side of town; a Longjing village detour would make a different day.

Choose roughly 3–5 km of walking. Skip this trip if we would rather have another Shanghai food day; all the original Shanghai pins still fit without it.

Same-day return & what to book

Line 2 takes us from People’s Square to Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station. Look for a high-speed service to Hangzhou East 杭州东站, then a taxi to the north lake shore. Compare a service to central Hangzhou station 杭州站 if its total journey works better.

Allow roughly 2½–3 hours each way from the hotel to the lake, including station travel, security, a suitable approximately one-hour train and the Hangzhou transfer. This is a planning allowance; check actual trains on 12306.

Train release: start checking 12 October for 26 October and book the return too. The ordinary public lakeshore/Bai Causeway walk does not need an attraction ticket. Any boat, paid garden or museum is separate; boats are optional and weather-dependent.

Keep our bags and Shanghai hotel room. This is a day trip, with no hotel change.

Before we go

A little preparation, an easier trip.

Booking checklist for these dates
  • Before October: verify both 12306 passenger profiles; ask the hotel about Ren He Guan’s booking policy. If keeping Lvbo Lang lunch, confirm the current main restaurant, menu and reservation.
  • From 7 October: check Suzhou trains for 21 October and book both directions when released.
  • From 12 October: check Hangzhou trains for 26 October if keeping the optional trip.
  • Before 21 October: reserve Humble Administrator’s Garden through its official platform. If choosing Suzhou Museum instead, start checking its current seven-day inclusive calendar from 15 October.
  • Before visiting: buy the correct People’s Square exhibition ticket if wanted, check Yu Garden’s daytime ticket, and confirm the tower package and passport flow.
  • Near departure: complete the China arrival card with final flight/hotel details and save all confirmations offline.

Release calendars and entry rules can change. A date in this checklist is a prompt to check availability, not a confirmed booking. Nothing has been purchased or reserved.

Passports, entry & arrival card

For this holiday, Malaysian and Singaporean ordinary passport holders can currently enter China visa-free for up to 30 days. Malaysian travellers also have a limit of 90 visa-free days within 180 days and need at least six months’ passport validity.

Keep your return flight and hotel confirmation available offline. Bring the original passports for hotel check-in, trains and museum visits. The hotel handles its guests’ accommodation registration.

  1. Use the official NIA arrival-card page or the WeChat mini program 移民局12367.
  2. Have passport details, flight information and the hotel’s exact Chinese address ready. Complete the requested fields and keep the confirmation available on your phone.
  3. The official arrival card is free. If online submission fails, paper cards and facilities to complete it are available on arrival.

Train tickets for day trips
  1. Create a 12306 account and add both travellers using names and details exactly as shown on their passports. Complete identity verification early; manual review can take 3–5 working days.
  2. Choose the correct departure and destination stations. For Suzhou’s old town, look for 苏州站 (Suzhou), checking the full journey from the hotel.
  3. Tickets normally go on sale 15 days including the travel day: check from 14 days before the trip, at the departure station’s release time.
  4. Book the return journey too. Carry the same original passports; use a staffed lane if the gate cannot read them. Leave time for station security.

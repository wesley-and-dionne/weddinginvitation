22THU

A whole day in old Suzhou

Breakfast to lantern-lit canals · back to Shanghai tonight

Suzhou station → taxi → Yu Xing Ji breakfast → Humble Administrator’s Garden → Pingjiang lanes → taxi → Shantang Street → Suzhou station

Our one full heritage day: a classical garden, canal-side houses, stone bridges, local food and evening lights. Take a morning train early enough to have breakfast in Suzhou; book the return after dinner and a short night walk.

Breakfast · a Suzhou noodle bowl

From Suzhou station, take a taxi to 裕兴记（西北街店），西北街11号. Choose a current pork or crab topping; the famous three-shrimp noodles are a summer speciality, so don’t build October around them. Walk east along Xibei Street and Dongbei Street towards the garden, roughly 10–20 minutes depending on the entry route.

Morning · give the garden room to breathe

拙政园 is a private scholar’s garden founded in 1509. Spend roughly 2–3 hours here if it holds our interest. The central pond is the heart of the visit: pause at 远香堂, look through the round openings at 梧竹幽居, and find the covered bridge 小飞虹. Follow the water and framed views instead of racing every pavilion.

Use the visitor-centre map or ask about an official guide/audio guide. Looking through a doorway, window or corridor at a deliberately framed scene makes the architecture much more interesting. Garden + museum + several temples would squeeze out the slow part of the day.

Lunch · choose buns or a small Suzhou spread

My first choice: 哑巴生煎（临顿路店）, around 15–25 minutes’ walk south of the garden, then turn east through the lanes to Pingjiang Road. Share a small portion of sweet, crisp-bottomed pork buns and add wonton soup.

If its queue is long: go straight to Pingjiang Road for 桃花源记（平江1店）: chicken-broth wontons, one small savoury dish and a shared red-bean dessert. Convenient and varied, but the reviews are mixed; there is no reason to queue at both.

Afternoon · canals, tea & old lanes

Wander along Pingjiang Road, turning into Daruxiang and Zhongzhangjia Lane for smaller bridges and quieter views. The main street is lively and commercial; the side lanes are a better place to slow down.

Choose one seated break: a short hand-rowed canal boat ride, or tea with pingtan — local storytelling and singing accompanied by strings. Ask a teahouse what is actually on: a short musical selection is easier to enjoy than a long story in Suzhou dialect. A plain tea stop is equally good.

Sweet stop: share 赤豆糊糖粥 or 红豆沙小圆子 at 桃花源记 if we didn’t eat there at lunch. Leave appetite for dinner.

Dinner · Suzhou classics, then Shantang lights

Take a taxi from an accessible road near Pingjiang to the Shantang area; allow roughly 20–35 minutes including pickup and traffic. Walk into the pedestrian street for 松鹤楼（山塘街店），山塘街196–198号. Reserve dinner if possible. For two, choose a small 松鼠桂鱼 plus greens and rice, or choose 响油鳝糊 and 蟹粉豆腐 instead of a whole fish.

After dinner, enjoy the canal reflections and lanterns around Shantang Bridge. Keep to a short section of the old street; there is no need to walk all seven li to Tiger Hill. Then taxi to Suzhou station, or use Line 2 from Shantang Street to Suzhou Railway Station, two stops.

Plan for about 5–7 km spread over the day, plus taxis. This is our fullest outing. If we linger in the garden or at tea, use the shorter version below and keep dinner in Suzhou.

Bridges, open river views & the skyline after sunset

Zhapu Road Bridge → Waibaidu Bridge → North Bund promenade / Manner → Little Giant Egg

Start with the bridges while there is daylight, then follow the river east into North Bund. This gives us a different angle from the main Bund: old waterfront buildings, the river bend and Pudong’s towers in the same broad view.

  1. 乍浦路桥: frame Suzhou Creek with Waibaidu Bridge and the skyline beyond. This is the approach to North Bund, not the main riverside promenade.

Breakfast: 张师傅早餐店（石门二路487号） for 粢饭团 and soy milk. Use a short taxi; keep it small if having the crab lunch.

Lunch: 成隆行蟹王府（九江路店） if we want the crab upgrade; ask for à-la-carte noodles rather than committing to a banquet. Then taxi to Zhapu Road Bridge for the photo route. Yun Lai Ji temple noodles remain the lighter alternative, subject to kitchen hours.

Photo notes & access

The public bridges and promenade need no sightseeing reservation. Paid rooftop viewpoints and café terraces have their own entry policies, so the plan works from ground level.

Take a daylight frame, then another after the lights appear. On a phone, keep both people and the skyline in the normal camera view, tap to expose for the bright buildings, and hold still. Avoid flash against a distant skyline.

The North Bund photo points are identified in current Hongkou district reports. “Better” is a photographic preference: this angle suits skyline portraits; the main Bund on 20 October still gives the classic building-lined promenade.

Shanghai Wild Animal Park

A whole weekday for animals · easy dinner back in town

Hotel → Shanghai Wild Animal Park → hotel / dinner

A simple park plan

  1. Arrive in the morning: collect the current park map and check the drive-through boarding point, queue and last service. Head there early if the wait is reasonable.
  2. Take the standard drive-through bus: this is the main way to see the animal ranges. Visitors repeatedly recommend the ordinary bus; upgraded feeding vehicles are optional.
  3. Lunch and a sit-down break: use a park restaurant near our route rather than walking across the grounds for food.
  4. Choose favourite walking exhibits: pandas, red pandas, giraffes and the other habitats we most want to see. Pick a manageable loop from the park map; add the boat only if we want another ride and its queue is short.

Breakfast: hotel breakfast or Xiao Yang before setting off; check opening hours.

Lunch: practical park food. The official site lists McDonald’s and Master Kong beef noodles; follow that day’s park map and service notices. These are convenience choices, not a special food recommendation.

Dinner: 很久以前羊肉串（南京路第一百货店）, near our hotel: lamb skewers, aubergine and corn, ordered gradually. Check the queue after the park; De Xing remains the quick fallback if we are tired.

Allow most of the day, including the long transfers and several kilometres of walking. Current post–National Day opening hours are 9am–5pm; do not rely on summer night-park hours. Follow any earlier last-entry or ride cutoff.

Park ticket, passports & transport

Book: use the park’s official website → ticket button, or verified WeChat 上海野生动物园 (ID shysdwy). Choose 26 October and the admission product. The official ticket shop is linked from the park site.

Check the live product for the standard bus inclusion, entry method and refunds; feeding buses, boat rides and electric transport can have separate charges. Check that the booking form accepts both passports. If unclear, ask our hotel to call 021-58036000 and confirm the foreign-passport purchase/entry method. Bring originals and saved ticket confirmations.

Easiest: hotel-arranged taxi or DiDi to 上海野生动物园，浦东新区南六公路178号. Allow roughly 60–90 minutes each way, longer in traffic; arrange or check the return pickup point before leaving the park.

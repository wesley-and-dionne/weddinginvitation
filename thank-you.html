Parking

The hotel provides on-site parking at a flat rate of RM15 for wedding guests. Please check with the wedding reception team for ticket validation.

Getting There by Rail

  • Monorail: Raja Chulan station, approximately a 3-minute walk.
  • LRT: KLCC station on the Kelana Jaya Line, approximately 450 metres away.
  • MRT: Bukit Bintang station on the Kajang Line, approximately 720 metres away. From the nearby Bukit Bintang Monorail station, ride one stop towards Titiwangsa to Raja Chulan.

Our Love Language Is Food: A Taste of KL

Being long-distance means we have travelled many miles for each other, usually with a meal planned at the other end. These are our carefully curated KL favourites.

Dionne’s Favourites

  • Menya Appare

    Must order: yuzu shio ramen.

    The Gardens Mall. Other branches available. Google Maps

  • Restoran Darling

    Dionne’s favourite char kuey teow: properly savoury, full of wok hei, and definitely not its sweeter Singaporean cousin. No diplomatic incident intended.

    Puchong Google Maps

  • Ah Sang Bak Kut Teh

    For classic herbal bak kut teh with fragrant oil rice.

    Sungai Way, Petaling Jaya Google Maps

  • Big Big Crab

    Must order: black tofu, plus their great-value crabs.

    Kepong HQ Google Maps

  • Umi Ka Shin

    A reasonably priced taste of the Toyosu Market experience.

    Bukit Damansara Google Maps

  • TalkCrab

    Go for the marinated raw crab when quality matters.

    Kepong. Subang Jaya branch also available. Google Maps

  • Kona Bakehouse

    Must order: original butter shiopan and yuzu butter.

    SS2, Petaling Jaya Google Maps

  • KLCG

    For the best croissant rolls and mochi cake.

    Ampang Park. Other KL branches available. Google Maps

  • Four Beans

    Must order: plain tau fu fah with osmanthus syrup, and aiyu jelly with rose lychee jelly topping.

    Cheras Traders Square. Other branches available. Google Maps
